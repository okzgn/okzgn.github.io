About

This is the applied AI, platform and software engineering practice of Elías Francisco Alvarado Soshina. The name dates from 2019; the work behind it, from more than twenty years of designing and building software.

Table of Contents

    The Name

    The name was found in code. In 2019, while Elías was building the hosting platform, a JavaScript function he wrote used five variables: O, K, Z, G and N. Seen together, they looked like an original name, and it sounded like oxygen.

    Tagline

    Every byte matters.

    A byte is the smallest thing a system stores or sends, and the easiest to waste. The tagline says that nothing is too small to count: every byte of code, memory, traffic or tokens is weighed against what it gives back. That care is what keeps software fast on modest devices, affordable at scale and simple to maintain.

    Core Values

    • Understand it to the core. A problem is broken down to its parts before anything is built. When no existing tool fit, Elías wrote his own: a CSS selector engine, a web server, a query language.
    • Lean by design. Few dependencies, and nothing to maintain that does not earn its place. Estructura and Componentes have no dependencies, the 2019 hosting platform needs no database, and this website has no build step.
    • Proven, not promised. Benchmarks, tests, fuzzing and security audits ship with the code, as in Turbo, the write-ahead-log ingestion engine and the benchmarks in the articles. Results are measured too: cloud costs cut by 70% and LLM inference costs by 50% in recent projects.
    • Secure by default. Least-privilege access, per-tenant sandboxing, crash-resilient storage and HTTPS everywhere, with automated SSL certificates, are the starting point.
    • One craft, end to end. Design and code are one discipline, handled by the same developer from start to finish. Every project ends in a documented handoff: tagged repositories, reproducible builds and architecture notes the client owns.

    Target Audience

    • Startups and product teams that need senior architecture without a full platform team: an MVP that has to scale, a first AI feature, a stack decision that will last.
    • Small and mid-sized businesses that want to automate operations with AI agents, move daily work to cloud tools such as point of sale or ticketing, and keep infrastructure costs under control.
    • Enterprises and engineering teams with specialized needs: high-throughput AI services, open-source models served on private GPUs, technical audits, DevSecOps and FinOps.
    • Agencies and software companies that need a technical partner behind their own brand: hosting, performance work and white-label platforms.
    • Organizations with large file archives that need to store, search and extract information at multi-terabyte scale.

    What they share: they want software they own, results they can measure, and systems that run without a heavy stack.

    Two Decades of Building

    Born in Ukraine in 1991 and raised in Ecuador, Elías started with drawing and painting, and taught himself programming. His path runs from art and graphic design to platform engineering and applied AI: more than twenty years of building software that people use.

    From a first job in his early teens and a commercial platform at 16 to his own web server, frameworks and applied AI, the path is told year by year in the full history.
