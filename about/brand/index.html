Brand Guidelines

How the brand looks and is written: the logo, color palette, typography and design system behind the website, so that every page, document and product reads as one brand.

Table of Contents

    Name and Tagline

    In text, the name is always written in capitals, as one word: OKZGN. Lowercase appears only in handles and addresses, such as github.com/okzgn. Where it comes from is told in The Name.

    Every byte matters.

    The tagline is written in sentence case, with a final period: only the first word is capitalized. What it means is told in Tagline.

    Logo

    The logotype is drawn on a geometric grid, not set in a typeface: one stroke width, equal letter widths, rounded outer corners and a G with its bar cut at a fixed angle. The O sets the proportions: its diameter is the cap height times the golden ratio, drawn as two concentric rings around an open center. On its own, the O is the symbol, used as the favicon and app icon.

    • Brand Blue on light backgrounds; white on dark ones. Never orange, gradients, shadows or outlines.
    • Clear space on every side at least as wide as one ring of the O.
    • Never stretched, rotated or retyped in a font.
    • Where the full logotype would be under 24 px tall, the symbol is used alone.

    Color Palette

    Neutrals carry most of the page, blue gives the identity, and orange marks only actions and selections: roughly 70, 20 and 10 percent.

    • Brand Blue#004E9BLogo, headings, links, primary buttons.
    • Bright Blue#0065CAActive and completed states, status marks, reading progress.
    • Deep Blue#002447Base of dark-mode surfaces.
    • Sky#B8DBFFThe brand blue in dark mode.
    • Signal Orange#F26522Accent: calls to action, hover and selected states.
    • Burnt Orange#C6460COrange text and buttons on light backgrounds.
    • Ink#000D1AText in light mode, background in dark mode.
    • Paper#F4F4F5Background in light mode, text in dark mode.

    Typography

    AaNoto SansRegular and SemiBold. Headings, interface and body text.
    AaNoto Sans MonoRegular. Code, data, labels and figures.
    Display and headings
    SemiBold with tight tracking (−0.03 em on display titles, −0.01 em on headings). Display titles scale from 40 to 72 px, section titles from 30 to 44 px.
    Body
    Regular at 16 to 17 px, with a line height of 1.6, or 1.7 for long reading.
    Labels
    13 px with slightly open tracking (+0.04 em).

    Design System

    Tokens
    Every color, shadow, radius, size and duration is a named token; the light and dark themes are the same tokens with different values.
    Surfaces
    White cards on Paper in light mode, deep-blue cards on Ink in dark mode, with a 1 px hairline, a soft shadow and a faint top highlight. Blue-tinted and orange-tinted variants mark featured content.
    Shape
    Corner radii on a fixed scale of 6, 8, 14, 20 and 28 px; buttons, tags and badges are pills.
    Spacing
    A 4 px base scale (4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48 px), content up to 1200 px wide, and reading columns of about 72 characters.
    Icons
    Line icons with round ends, in two families with one stroke weight each: large icons for services, articles and projects, drawn on an 80-unit canvas, and interface icons for the menu, footer and table of contents, drawn on a 24-unit grid. They come in Brand Blue, or Burnt Orange in their warm variant, and lighten in dark mode (#8AC4FF and #FF962E). Small interface icons take the color of the text around them.
    Motion
    Short transitions (about 0.2 s) with a slight spring on presses and checks. Every animation respects the reduced-motion setting.
