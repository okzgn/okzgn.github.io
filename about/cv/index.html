    Professional Profile

    Senior software developer, building production systems since 2007 and focused on applied AI since 2024: autonomous agents, high-throughput inference pipelines, and multi-tenant, multilingual platforms. He combines architecture and full-stack development with infrastructure, DevOps and cloud cost optimization (FinOps), and writes much of his own tooling as open source.

    Through OKZGN, which he founded in 2019, he designs, builds and runs every layer of a digital product, from AI agents and architecture to web and mobile apps and infrastructure, and leads every engagement himself, from the first conversation to the handoff.
