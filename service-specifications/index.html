    Service Specifications

    This document details the operational scope, required client inputs, technical delivery standards, handoff formats, realistic turnaround times, and known edge-case constraints across the OKZGN service catalog. Every engagement is executed under strict software engineering principles: reproducible environments, deterministic delivery, and hardened infrastructure.

    Standard Engagement Protocol

    To avoid scope creep, technical friction, and operational bottlenecks, all projects follow an established four-stage intake and delivery lifecycle:

    • 1. Discovery & Technical Pre-flight: Before sending credentials, source code, or payments, consultation is conducted directly via official channels. Exact milestones are defined, system compatibility is verified, and mutual NDAs are executed if proprietary data is involved.
    • 2. Zero-Trust Access Provisioning: Root credentials, shared master passwords, or personal account tokens must never be transmitted over plain chat. Public SSH keys, GPG keys, or specific IAM permission scopes (least-privilege model) are provisioned for staging and production environments.
    • 3. Iterative Execution & Staging: Implementations are executed in isolated staging branches or dedicated sandboxes with verifiable benchmarks and reproducible build pipelines.
    • 4. Final Handoff & Knowledge Transfer: Production sign-off includes tagged Git repositories, infrastructure-as-code manifests, architecture documentation (Markdown), and direct handover support.

    1. Subscription Services (Managed & SaaS)

    Storage Search

    Scope of Work: Deployment and maintenance of high-throughput indexing engines capable of parsing, vectorizing, and searching across multi-terabyte unorganized document archives, databases, and media repositories.

    • Required Client Inputs: Bucket access (S3/Cloudflare R2/GCS read-only credentials), target file extensions (.pdf, .docx, .json, .csv, media), search parameter requirements (full-text lexical vs. dense vector semantic), and estimated monthly query frequency.
    • Deliverables & Formats: Private REST/gRPC search API endpoint, secure Web Dashboard for team queries, API documentation (OpenAPI 3.0), and index telemetry health checks.
    • Contact Point: Coordinate with the lead engineer to calculate initial ingestion time and required worker node sizing based on dataset entropy.
    • Estimated Timelines: Initial cluster provisioning: 2–3 business days. Full index ingestion: 12 to 72 hours per terabyte, subject to upstream read limits.
    • Caveats & Operational Constraints: Corrupted or password-protected files fail silently into an error log. Ingestion speed is directly throttled by the client's cloud egress limits.

    Storage Link

    Scope of Work: High-capacity, cost-controlled cloud and edge storage architecture optimized for continuous file archiving, asset distribution, and multi-terabyte data preservation.

    • Required Client Inputs: Desired storage quota (TB), regional residency requirements (US/EU/LATAM), retention and lifecycle policies, and custom domain/subdomain pointers (CNAME records).
    • Deliverables & Formats: High-speed S3-compatible API credentials, authenticated Web portal access, automated backup lifecycle scripts, and bandwidth consumption reporting.
    • Contact Point: Contact via WhatsApp/Email to finalize egress allowances and backup replication frequency.
    • Estimated Timelines: Dedicated tenant provisioning and DNS certificate propagation: 24 to 48 hours.
    • Caveats & Operational Constraints: Bandwidth is metered on excess bursts beyond agreed allocation tiers; cold-storage tiers may exhibit 2–5 minute latency spikes for archival retrieval.

    Point of Sale (POS)

    Scope of Work: Provisioning, configuration, and maintenance of a real-time cloud-synchronized point of sale system with offline transactional durability, inventory control, and multi-terminal sync.

    • Required Client Inputs: Inventory catalog in structured format (CSV, Excel, or JSON), tax brackets and local fiscal requirements, cashier privilege levels, and hardware specs (thermal receipt printers, barcode scanners, cash drawers).
    • Deliverables & Formats: Cloud tenant URL, PWA client for mobile/desktop, offline sync service worker configuration, raw SQL/CSV catalog import, and staff training documentation.
    • Contact Point: Submit inventory sample files to the lead engineer to verify SKU formatting and relational category schemas.
    • Estimated Timelines: Tenant configuration and data ingestion: 3–5 business days. On-premise hardware pairing: 1–2 business days.
    • Caveats & Operational Constraints: Thermal printer integration relies on standard ESC/POS or network drivers; offline transactions resolve deterministically upon reconnection using vector clocks, which may require manual resolution if two registers sell the identical last physical unit simultaneously.

    Timed-Rate Tickets

    Scope of Work: Deployment of an automated, schedule-based rate calculation and access ticketing platform for venues, parking facilities, timed rentals, and coworking spaces.

    • Required Client Inputs: Mathematical fee schedules (grace periods, tiered hourly rates, overnight penalties, holiday exceptions), physical terminal count, gate controller webhook endpoints (if hardware-coupled).
    • Deliverables & Formats: Real-time administrative dashboard, operator terminal view, printable QR/Barcode layout templates, automated end-of-day revenue audit reports.
    • Contact Point: Review pricing tables with OKZGN engineering to identify cyclical edge cases (such as daylight saving shifts or midnight roll-overs).
    • Estimated Timelines: Rate engine configuration and staging validation: 3–6 business days.
    • Caveats & Operational Constraints: Lost ticket handling requires administrative override privileges; physical barriers require dry-contact relay controllers with reliable local network connectivity.

    2. Applied Artificial Intelligence

    Advanced Prompt Engineering

    Scope of Work: Empirical optimization of system prompts, meta-instructions, context windows, and output guardrails to achieve deterministic structure, zero evasion, and high fidelity from foundation LLMs.

    • Required Client Inputs: Minimum 50–100 real-world input examples with desired ground-truth outputs, failure logs from existing prompts, and target LLM vendor/version constraints (OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, Llama).
    • Deliverables & Formats: Version-controlled prompt engineering suite (Markdown/YAML files), JSON Schema output definitions, automated eval test harness (Python/TypeScript), and precision benchmark report.
    • Contact Point: Schedule a technical intake call with the lead engineer to review edge cases and domain-specific terminology.
    • Estimated Timelines: 3 to 7 business days depending on dataset volume and heuristic complexity.
    • Caveats & Operational Constraints: Upstream foundation model updates by vendors may introduce minor behavioral drift; prompts are tuned against fixed model checkpoints to guarantee stability.

    Autonomous Multimodal Agents

    Scope of Work: Engineering autonomous task-oriented agents capable of parsing text, PDFs, audio, and visual data, equipped with secure external tool invocation (APIs, databases, headless web browsers).

    • Required Client Inputs: End-to-end procedural workflow map, sandboxed API credentials for all external tools the agent must invoke, explicit boundary conditions (what the agent is strictly prohibited from doing), and communication channels (WhatsApp, Slack, Webhooks).
    • Deliverables & Formats: Full agent application source code (Node.js, Python, or Go), containerized deployment setup (Docker), persistent conversational memory layer, and real-time execution audit logs.
    • Contact Point: Technical consultation with the lead engineer to specify state-machine states and fallback routing before writing agent runtime code.
    • Estimated Timelines: Simple single-tool agents: 5–10 business days. Complex multi-step, multi-agent swarms: 3–6 weeks.
    • Caveats & Operational Constraints: Autonomous execution requires strict loop-breaker mechanisms and maximum budget token caps to avoid recursive runaways caused by malformed third-party API payloads.

    High-Throughput AI Architecture

    Scope of Work: Design and implementation of high-concurrency inference proxies, queuing systems, and asynchronous pipelines designed to handle hundreds or thousands of concurrent model requests without dropping connections.

    • Required Client Inputs: Peak concurrent user projections (RPS/QPS), payload profiles (average token length, image dimensions), latency SLAs (Time to First Token, total request duration), and cloud/hardware infrastructure budgets.
    • Deliverables & Formats: Reverse proxy and worker pool architecture (Go, Redis, RabbitMQ, or Celery), Server-Sent Events (SSE) streaming infrastructure, Locust/k6 benchmark load scripts, and Prometheus/Grafana metric collectors.
    • Contact Point: Direct architecture review with OKZGN platform engineering to evaluate edge vs. centralized compute topology.
    • Estimated Timelines: 2 to 4 weeks for architecture design, stress testing, and staging rollout.
    • Caveats & Operational Constraints: Total system throughput is fundamentally governed by hardware GPU VRAM bounds or external API provider rate limits (TPM/RPM tiers).

    Custom Model Deployment

    Scope of Work: Production-ready deployment of open-source models (Llama, Mistral, Whisper, Stable Diffusion) on private cloud GPU instances or dedicated bare-metal servers using optimized inference runtimes.

    • Required Client Inputs: Model identifier (HuggingFace repository or fine-tuned checkpoint weights), target hardware access (AWS EC2, RunPod, Lambda Labs, Hetzner GPU, or on-premise CUDA servers), and internal networking/VPC specifications.
    • Deliverables & Formats: Hardened vLLM, TGI, or Triton inference container, OpenAI-compatible HTTP/gRPC endpoint, systemd service units, and automatic warm-up health-check scripts.
    • Contact Point: Consult with the lead engineer to calculate exact VRAM sizing, quantization tradeoffs (FP16 vs. AWQ/GPTQ 4-bit), and context window allocation.
    • Estimated Timelines: 3 to 7 business days per model architecture.
    • Caveats & Operational Constraints: Dedicated GPU hardware availability is volatile across public cloud providers; instances must be secured and provisioned prior to contract start.

    AI Costs & Reliability

    Scope of Work: Implementation of cost-reduction layers, semantic prompt/response caching, fallback routing, and token usage governance to minimize vendor billing while eliminating downstream downtime.

    • Required Client Inputs: 30–60 days of itemized billing records, API gateway request logs, and acceptable quality degradation thresholds for fallback models.
    • Deliverables & Formats: Dynamic semantic caching layer (Redis / vector index), dynamic multi-vendor fallback gateway, cost attribution telemetry dashboards, and optimization audit report.
    • Contact Point: Share anonymized request/response telemetry with OKZGN to project quantifiable return on investment.
    • Estimated Timelines: 1 to 2 weeks for audit, proxy deployment, and cache tuning.
    • Caveats & Operational Constraints: Semantic cache hit rates are directly correlated with prompt uniformity; high-entropy, completely unique queries bypass cache layers as intended.

    5. Infrastructure & Hosting

    Domain Management

    Scope of Work: End-to-end domain lifecycle administration: registration, registrar transfers, DNSSEC enforcement, nameserver routing, and enterprise mail authentication records (SPF, DKIM, DMARC).

    • Required Client Inputs: Registrar access or authorization transfer codes (EPP keys), required mail host destinations, and domain mapping records.
    • Deliverables & Formats: Fully configured DNS zone file, verified DNSSEC cryptographic signing, 100% email deliverability test score (SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass), and renewal calendar.
    • Contact Point: Provide temporary delegated access to registrar directly to lead engineer.
    • Estimated Timelines: DNS configuration: 1–2 hours. Global root DNS propagation: up to 24–48 hours depending on registry TTL.
    • Caveats & Operational Constraints: Registrar domain transfer locks and mandatory 60-day ICANN post-registration holds cannot be bypassed.

    High-Performance Hosting

    Scope of Work: Provisioning and operational tuning of bare-metal and dedicated VPS instances engineered for zero-overhead, sub-50ms server response times (TTFB), and hardened Linux environments.

    • Required Client Inputs: Web application technology stack, projected monthly unique visitors, geographic distribution of traffic, and raw database storage sizing.
    • Deliverables & Formats: Dedicated server environment, custom web server config (Nginx/Turbo/Caddy), automated database backup pipelines, and server health monitoring hooks.
    • Contact Point: Contact the lead engineer to calculate memory, NVMe disk, and compute core allocations.
    • Estimated Timelines: 1 to 3 business days for full server provisioning and migration.
    • Caveats & Operational Constraints: Hardware configurations require scheduled maintenance windows for kernel updates, coordinated during off-peak traffic hours.

    Professional Server Solutions

    Scope of Work: Enterprise cloud infrastructure architecture on AWS, GCP, Azure, Hetzner, or private data centers, including Virtual Private Clouds (VPC), private subnets, bastion gateways, and automated elasticity.

    • Required Client Inputs: Cloud provider account root/organization access, budget constraints, security compliance mandates (HIPAA, SOC2, GDPR), and architectural topology requirements.
    • Deliverables & Formats: Infrastructure as Code (Terraform or OpenTofu scripts), network topology diagrams, automated backup replication systems, and environment access credentials.
    • Contact Point: Direct engineering consultation to review infrastructure cost modeling and high-availability architecture.
    • Estimated Timelines: 1 to 3 weeks for full cloud infrastructure provisioning.
    • Caveats & Operational Constraints: Cloud platform service quotas (e.g., initial vCPU limits on new AWS/GCP accounts) require advance provider ticket requests.

    Security First

    Scope of Work: Defense-in-depth platform hardening: Web Application Firewall (WAF) deployment, automated DDoS mitigation, brute-force IP throttling (Fail2ban/iptables), and remediation of top OWASP vulnerabilities.

    • Required Client Inputs: Target domain names, server IP addresses, ingress traffic schemas, and list of any known prior security incidents or ongoing attack vectors.
    • Deliverables & Formats: Hardened firewall rulesets, edge proxy WAF configuration, rate-limiting rules, SSL/TLS cipher suites (A+ SSL Labs rating), and post-hardening penetration verification report.
    • Contact Point: Urgent or active incident remediation must be signaled immediately via WhatsApp for expedited triage.
    • Estimated Timelines: Standard hardening: 2–5 business days. Emergency DDoS mitigation: immediate deployment within hours.
    • Caveats & Operational Constraints: Aggressive WAF rules require an initial 24–48 hour passive observation mode to eliminate false-positive blocks on legitimate user API calls.

    SSL & DNS

    Scope of Work: Enterprise TLS/SSL certificate lifecycle automation, wildcard certificate deployments, HSTS preloading, and ultra-low-latency Anycast DNS architecture.

    • Required Client Inputs: DNS control panel credentials or zone delegation authority, list of all apex domains and subdomains requiring encryption.
    • Deliverables & Formats: Automated ACME certificate issuance scripts (Let's Encrypt / ZeroSSL), auto-renewing systemd timers, HSTS configuration, and zero-downtime DNS routing.
    • Contact Point: Transmit domain access securely to lead engineering.
    • Estimated Timelines: 1 business day.
    • Caveats & Operational Constraints: ACME certificate issuance relies on public CA verification challenges; rapid consecutive failed renewals can trigger temporary 168-hour Let's Encrypt rate limits.

    Scope of Work: Continuous systems administration for Linux distributions (Debian, Ubuntu, AlmaLinux, Alpine), managing kernel parameters, daemon process control, container orchestration, and automated storage snapshots.

    • Required Client Inputs: Dedicated SSH access with sudo privileges (public key only), server hardware provider panel access, and an inventory of all hosted background processes.

    • Contact Point: Assign dedicated server permissions to the lead engineer.
