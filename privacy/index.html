Privacy Statement

Zero tracking, no cookies, hardened infrastructure, and client-side memory execution by design.

Table of Contents

    Thanks for visiting the website and taking a moment to review privacy and security handling across platforms. As an engineering practice focused on high-performance architecture, applied AI, and clean software standards, OKZGN treats privacy as an architectural requirement, not an afterthought.

    Zero Tracking & No Cookies Policy

    Cookies of any kind are not used.

    • No tracking cookies, no session cookies, no profiling, and no third-party marketing beacons.
    • No embedded third-party widgets, analytics platforms, or social media scripts that track your browsing habits across the web.
    • No browser or device fingerprinting.

    Infrastructure & Delivery

    • Static & Deterministic Delivery: The frontend is delivered with standard web primitives (HTML5, CSS3, and modern vanilla JavaScript) over a hardened HTTPS configuration enforced with a strict Content Security Policy (CSP).
    • Edge & Proxy Security: When traffic routes through edge caching or proxy networks (such as Cloudflare or dedicated edge nodes), requests are processed strictly for delivery acceleration and security (mitigating DDoS and malicious automated attacks). No persistent user-tracking identifiers are attached to user visits or sessions.

    Client-Side Operations & Cart State

    Any dynamic state on this website (such as the on-page service cart or interface state) is handled strictly client-side, without transmitting or persisting unsubmitted cart interactions or form states to any remote database.

    • Cart Session: Selected services, the billing mode, and the optional client name are kept in the browser's sessionStorage on your device, so the cart survives page reloads. This data is discarded when the browser tab is closed and cleared after a successful inquiry submission.
    • Projects Cache: The open source projects page requests the public repository list from the GitHub API (api.github.com) directly from your browser, and caches the response in localStorage for up to 24 hours to avoid repeated requests. As with any direct request, GitHub receives standard connection metadata (such as IP address and user agent) under its own privacy policy.

    Direct Communication & Data Exchange

    When reaching out via direct channels:

    • You directly control what information is shared.
    • Project Inquiry Form: On submission, the entered details (name, work email, company, phone, service of interest, engagement model, and project details), along with the selected cart services, the page address, the referring page, the browser language, and a timestamp, are sent over HTTPS to inquiry.okzgn.com and used solely to evaluate and respond to the inquiry.
    • WhatsApp & Email: Messages sent through WhatsApp or email are also handled by those providers under their own privacy policies.
    • Any technical context, architectural requirements, or specifications provided are treated as strictly confidential and used solely to evaluate, scope, and execute your project or support inquiry.
    • Use pseudonyms or sanitized code samples if your organization requires strict privacy constraints.

    Server Logs & Maintenance

    Standard web server access logs (used strictly to monitor system availability, diagnose technical faults, and defend against malicious traffic) retain minimal technical metadata and are routinely rotated and purged, without correlating logs with personal identities.
