    Privacy Statement

    Thanks for visiting the website and taking a moment to review privacy and security handling across platforms. As an engineering practice focused on high-performance architecture, applied AI, and clean software standards, privacy is an architectural requirement, not an afterthought.

    Zero Tracking & No Cookies Policy

    Cookies of any kind are not used.

    • No tracking cookies, no session cookies, no profiling, and no third-party marketing beacons.
    • No embedded third-party widgets, analytics platforms, or social media scripts that track your browsing habits across the web.
    • No browser or device fingerprinting.

    Infrastructure & Delivery

    • Static & Deterministic Delivery: The frontend is delivered with standard web primitives (HTML5, CSS3, and modern vanilla JavaScript) over a hardened HTTPS configuration enforced with a strict Content Security Policy (CSP).
    • Edge & Proxy Security: When traffic routes through edge caching or proxy networks (such as Cloudflare or dedicated edge nodes), requests are processed strictly for delivery acceleration and security (mitigating DDoS and malicious automated attacks). No persistent user-tracking identifiers are attached to the user visits or sessions.

    Client-Side Operations & Cart State

    Any dynamic state on this website (such as the on-page service cart or interface state) is handled strictly client-side in the user's device memory, without transmitting or persisting unsubmitted cart interactions or form states to any remote database.

    Direct Communication & Data Exchange

    When reaching out via direct channels (email at hello@okzgn.com or WhatsApp):

    • You directly control what information is shared.
    • Any technical context, architectural requirements, or specifications provided are treated as strictly confidential and used solely to evaluate, scope, and execute your project or support inquiry.
    • Use pseudonyms or sanitized code samples if your organization requires strict privacy constraints.

    Server Logs & Maintenance

    Standard web server access logs (used strictly to monitor system availability, diagnose technical faults, and defend against malicious traffic) retain minimal technical metadata and are routinely purged/rotated, without correlating logs with identifiable personal identities.
