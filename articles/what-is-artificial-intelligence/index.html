What is artificial intelligence?

The conceptual reality of modern AI: how statistical patterns in high-dimensional vector spaces are condensed into relational numerical layers for probabilistic inference.

Table of Contents

    The Conceptual Reality of Modern AI

    Modern artificial intelligence is the process of organizing, filtering, and synthesizing statistically relevant patterns within data transformed into numerical vectors. These patterns are condensed into relational numerical layers used to compute and infer the probabilistic outcomes that most closely approximate what was synthesized, based on information received and transformed into the same numerical data structure.
